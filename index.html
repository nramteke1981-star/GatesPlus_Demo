5Four Phase 1 servicesClick a service to see its complete sub-feature scope
Legal adviceRequest → lawyer → consultation → advice → closure
  • Property-linked or standalone legal request
  • Category, jurisdiction, urgency, consent and document capture
  • Disclaimer acceptance, fee rule and unique case ID
  • Lawyer eligibility and conflict-of-interest check
  • Eligible lawyer assignment and chat, call or video booking
  • Secure case messages, meeting link, notes and attachments
  • Document review or drafting with comments and version history
  • Final advice/document release, case tracking and closure summary
Leasing and rentalList → search → visit → agreement → handover
  • Approved rent/lease listing with terms and availability
  • Search and shortlist by city, locality, type, price, area and amenities
  • Customer enquiry and conflict-free site-visit booking
  • CRM/agent assignment, contact and follow-up tracking
  • Offer, counter-offer, acceptance, rejection, withdrawal and expiry
  • Tenant, ownership and agreed rental checks
  • Approved agreement template, preview, legal review and version control
  • Optional eSign, invoice, payment, receipt and refund tracking
  • Visible Available, Reserved, Rented/Leased or Unavailable status
  • Block overlapping or simultaneous allotment of the same unit
  • Deposit, keys, inventory, meter readings and party acknowledgements
Property protectionEnrol → schedule → visit → alert → report
  • Verified property enrolment and protection-plan selection
  • Visit frequency, request details and supporting evidence
  • CRM planning and assignment to an available field executive
  • Visit start, time, location, checklist, photos and observations
  • Immediate portal update for suspected encroachment or site risk
  • Approved call or WhatsApp contact with recorded outcome
  • Immediate owner and supervisor alert for serious findings
  • Optional linked legal request with customer consent
  • Approved report, next visit/follow-up and complete protection history
Buy/Sell of flat or plotList → enquire → verify → negotiate → facilitate closure
  • Approved sale listing by an authorised owner or agent
  • Flat/plot details, expected price and seller authority documents
  • Buyer search, shortlist, enquiry and site-visit request
  • CRM/agent coordination, feedback and next-action tracking
  • Offer, counter-offer, acceptance, rejection, withdrawal and expiry
  • Ownership, authority, encumbrance and agreed legal checks
  • Block progression when an ownership mismatch or legal concern is unresolved
  • Public-notice details and objection period where legally required
  • Service charges, sale-document checklist and handover evidence
  • Buyer/seller confirmation and facilitator disclaimer before closure
  • Record later third-party claims without GatedPlus deciding title or settlement

Milestone: All four service journeys work end to end on the website.
